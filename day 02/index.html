Prithvi Narayan Shah

Today we will be talking about Prithivi Narayan Shah

Prithvi Narayan Shah (11 January 1723-11 January was) the monarch of the Gorkha Kingdom and the founding monarch of the Kingdom of Nepal.
He is credited with initaiting the Unification of Nepaland relocating Kathmandu as the royal capital.
Before Prithvi Narayan Shah's unification movement, there were total of 54 states in Nepal. The 54 states were named as baisi Raijya and chaubise Rajya.
In the South-Eastern Terai, there were three Sen states: Makawanpur, Bijayapur and Chaubandi.
